02 / Context

Shopping shouldn't require a size-chart investigation.

Brands publish different body-measurement ranges for the same labeled size. Comparing those guides can mean switching between tabs, units, and measurement formats.

What if those measurements were easier to compare side by side?

04 / Evidence II

Fit doesn't always match expectations.

31.4%

05 / The turn

The label is only the beginning.

A size number alone cannot tell the whole story. Shoppers deserve clear, comparable body measurements alongside that label.

06 / The ask

Make size charts easier to compare.

Ask apparel brands for consistent measurement units, clearly identified body measurements, and accessible side-by-side sizing information.
