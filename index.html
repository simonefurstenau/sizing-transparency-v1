01 / The context

Finding your size shouldn't feel like detective work.

Brands publish different body-measurement ranges for the same labeled size. To compare them, shoppers often have to switch between websites, measurement formats, and units.

But what happens when we put the numbers next to each other?

05 / The turn

The problem isn't the number. It's what the number leaves out.

A label can be a starting point, but shoppers need clear body measurements in consistent units to make meaningful comparisons.
